ITI Admission Process – Complete Step by Step Guide

ITI (Industrial Training Institute) admission process usually starts after 10th result declaration. Students who want technical skill-based education can apply for ITI courses.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum qualification: 10th pass
  • Age: Generally 14–40 years
  • Some trades require specific subjects

Admission Steps

  • Visit official state ITI website
  • Fill online application form
  • Upload required documents
  • Pay application fees
  • Participate in counseling

Documents Required

  • 10th marksheet
  • Passport size photo
  • Signature
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile certificate

Course Duration

ITI courses usually last 1 or 2 years depending on the trade selected.

Conclusion

ITI admission process is simple if you follow proper steps. Choose your trade carefully based on interest and career goals.
