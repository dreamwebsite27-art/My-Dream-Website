ITI COPA Admission 2026 – Complete Guide

ITI COPA (Computer Operator and Programming Assistant) ek popular vocational course hai jo computer field me career shuru karne ke liye best mana jata hai. Yeh course Directorate General of Training (DGT) ke under hota hai aur iski duration 1 saal hoti hai. 2026 me bhi ITI COPA admission ke liye students ka interest bahut zyada hai kyunki computer skills ki demand har field me badh rahi hai.

Is course me admission lene ke liye candidate ka 10th pass hona zaroori hai. Admission process state ke hisab se online ya offline ho sakta hai. Zyada tar states me merit ke basis par admission diya jata hai. Kuch institutes entrance exam bhi conduct karte hain.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10th pass hona zaroori
  • Basic computer knowledge hona helpful hai
  • Age limit state rules par depend karti hai

Course Duration

COPA course total 1 saal ka hota hai jisme 2 semesters hote hain. Isme theory aur practical dono shamil hote hain jisse students ko real computer work ka experience milta hai.

Admission Process

Admission lene ke liye aapko ITI official website par registration karna hota hai. Form fill karne ke baad documents upload karne hote hain aur merit list ka wait karna hota hai. Merit list me naam aane ke baad institute select kar sakte hain.

Course me kya sikhaya jata hai

  • Computer basics
  • MS Office
  • Internet usage
  • HTML aur web design
  • Programming basics
  • Cyber security basics

Future Scope

COPA karne ke baad aap computer operator, data entry operator, office assistant, web designer jaise roles me job kar sakte hain. Yeh course beginners ke liye best hai jo computer field me entry lena chahte hain.
