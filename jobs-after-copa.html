COPA Course ke Baad Best Jobs

ITI COPA course complete karne ke baad students ke paas kai job options available hote hain. Yeh course computer skills develop karta hai jo har industry me useful hote hain. Government aur private dono sectors me opportunities mil sakti hain.

Top Job Options

Computer Operator

Office me data handle karna, documents manage karna aur computer par daily tasks karna computer operator ki main responsibility hoti hai.

Data Entry Operator

Data entry jobs me companies ko digital records maintain karne ke liye operators ki zarurat hoti hai. Yeh beginners ke liye best job hoti hai.

Web Designer

HTML aur basic coding seekhne ke baad aap websites design kar sakte hain aur freelance work bhi kar sakte hain.

Office Assistant

Computer knowledge hone ki wajah se aap office assistant ke role me bhi kaam kar sakte hain.

Government Jobs

COPA ke baad government departments me bhi vacancies nikalti rehti hain jahan computer knowledge zaroori hota hai.

Salary

Starting salary 8,000 se 15,000 per month tak ho sakti hai. Experience ke sath salary badhti rehti hai.

Freelancing Opportunities

Online platforms par data entry, website designing aur typing work karke ghar baithe income generate ki ja sakti hai.
